BEATS
Beats are periodic fluctuations between maximum and minimum loudness when two sound waves of slightly different frequencies, travelling in the same direction, overlap each other.
Tuning forks give out pure notes (single frequency).
Same Frequency — No Beats
• If two tuning forks A and B of the same frequency, say 32 Hz, are sounded separately, they give out pure notes.

• If sounded simultaneously, it is difficult to differentiate one from the other.

• The sound waves are superposed and heard as a single pure note.
BEATS FORMATION
If tuning fork B is loaded with wax or plasticine, its frequency is lowered slightly — say it becomes 30 Hz.
Sounded together → a note of alternately increasing and decreasing intensity is heard.

This is called a beat note (or beat), due to interference between waves from A and B.
