Ripple Tank Diffraction Study
Apparatus Setup · Gap Width Comparison · Conclusion
TEXTBOOK · § RIPPLE TANK
APPARATUS COMPONENTS
  • Water inside a glass-bottomed tray
  • Vibrator (motorized oscillating bar/needle suspended by elastic bands)
  • Lamp / Light Source mounted on an overhead stand
  • White Screen placed below tray to view shadow wave patterns
  • Obstacles (rectangular blocks/barriers to form slits)
STUDY OF DIFFRACTION: GAP WIDTH EFFECT
OBSERVATIONS & CONCLUSION
Observation 1: When gap width is small compared to wavelength (d ≈ λ), diffraction is significant and waves bend around obstacles in a semicircular pattern.

Observation 2: As gap width increases relative to wavelength (d >> λ), diffraction decreases and waves pass through with minimal bending.
Conclusion: Demonstrates the qualitative effect of gap width on diffraction in a ripple tank, establishing that maximum diffraction requires gap size to be comparable to wavelength.
