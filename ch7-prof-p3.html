Superposition of Waves :-
Principle of Superposition & Phase Conditions
Principle Definition:
If particle of a medium is acted upon by 2 waves simultaneously, resultant (total) displacement of particle is equal to algebraic Sum of individual displacements.
Y = y₁ + y₂

• Y = Resultant Displacement
• y₁ = Displacement of 1st wave
• y₂ = Displacement of 2nd wave
Explanation (Same Phase):
When two overlapping waves have same phase (start up together).
If y₁ = y₂, then: Y = 2y₁ (Constructive)

Opposite Phase (Total Annihilation):
When two overlapping waves have opposite phase (one up, one down).
Y = y₁ − y₂
If y₁ = y₂, then: Y = 0 (Destructive Cancellation)
