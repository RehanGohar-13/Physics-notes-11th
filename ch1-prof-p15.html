CHAPTER NO 01
Rules of Zero: Decimals & Integers
• In case of decimal number zeros are significants.
• 20.0000
➔ all zeros significat
• 306.000
➔ also significant.
• In case of integers no. of significant zeros is determined by least count of measuring instrument
: 800 kg
: 10000000 g
Board Exam Least Count Rule Breakdown:
• For 800 kg:
  - If Least Count = 1 kg → 8.00 × 10² kg (3 sig figs)
  - If Least Count = 10 kg → 8.0 × 10² kg (2 sig figs)
  - If Least Count = 100 kg → 8 × 10² kg (1 sig fig)
• Always use Scientific Notation to remove ambiguity in integers!
