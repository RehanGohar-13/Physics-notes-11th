Superposition & Wave Function
Absent Day 1 · y = 0 case · n-wave sum · ANC
Destructive cancel (board):
If y₁ = y₂
y = 0
General Superposition (n waves):
If there are "n" number of waves having amplitude
y₁, y₂, y₃, … , yₙ

Then
y = y₁ + y₂ + y₃ + … + yₙ

Principle of superposition can also be written as
y(x,t) = y₁(x,t) + y₂(x,t) + … + yₙ(x,t)

y(x,t) is a wave function which means wave depends on position "x" in time "t".
3 Core Phenomena (board list):

1- Interference

Superposition of two waves having same direction and frequencies.

2- Stationary waves

Superposition of two waves of same frequencies but travelling in opposite direction.

3- Beat

Superposition of waves having same direction but slightly different frequency.

Noise cancellation Headphones :-
Our Headphone Captures ambient (environmental) noise and processing unit of headphones produces same signal in opposite direction. Both signal are played together and clear audio heard.
Board Check: ANC = destructive interference (phase shift ~180°). Same idea as y₁ + (−y₂) = 0.
