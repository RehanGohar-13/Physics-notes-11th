Stationary Waves in Microwave Oven (MCQs)
  • Microwaves: Wavelength range in mm – cm.
  • Oven is a metallic container.
  • Microwaves cannot escape through the metallic boundaries.
  • Possible to form stationary (standing) waves inside the oven.

Projectile Motion Range Formula (Side Note)
R = (vᵢ² · sin 2θ) / g
When θ = 45°:
R = [vᵢ² · sin 2(45°)] / g
R = (vᵢ² / g) · sin(90°)
R = vᵢ² / g   (Maximum Range)
★ Factor Note: The projectile range is maximized ONLY when the angle is exactly 45°. If the launch angle is greater or less than 45°, the value of sin(2θ) drops below 1, making the final range strictly smaller than Rmax.
