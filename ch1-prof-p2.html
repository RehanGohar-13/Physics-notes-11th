Dimension of Physical Quantity Core

DEFINITION

  • Dimensions are symbols that define the measurable properties of physical quantities.
  • They give the qualitative پہچان nature of physical quantities.

BASIC SYMBOLS

Length [L] Time [T] Mass [M] Temperature [θ] Electric current [A] Amount of substance [N] Intensity of light [J]
