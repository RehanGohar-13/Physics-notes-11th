Waves And Vibration (Chp #7)
Basic Definitions & Wave Diagram
Waves:
Waves are disturbances or variation in medium that transfers energy away from the source.
Disturbance:
Disturbance is vibration of Particle of medium that transfer energy because they move back and forth with also doing up and down.

Wave Types:

A- Sound Waves B- Water waves C- Seismic Waves (Earthquakes)
