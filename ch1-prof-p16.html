Special MCQs · Multiplication/Division & Addition/Subtraction Rules
Special MCQ's :- MCQ RULE
Rule: Power of 10 are not significant.

• In case of Multiplication and division.

= [ (102 × 10³) × (5.72 × 10³) ] / (1.333 × 10²)

= 6864000 / (1.333 × 10²)
• In case of Addition / Subtraction
  • Number of S.F are irrelevant.
  • Number of decimal places are required.
پوائنٹ کے بعد اتنے ہی نمبر آنے ہیں جتنے سوال میں سب سے کم نمبر ہیں (.) کے بعد۔
Board Exam Rule Summary:
• Multiplication / Division: Round the result to the fewest significant figures among the factors.
• Addition / Subtraction: Round the result to the fewest decimal places among the terms (number of total sig figs does not matter).
