Applications of Superposition
Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) Technology
Noise Cancellation Headphones:
Headphones that stop noise coming from the outside using the principle of destructive interference.
Working Mechanism:

1. Our headphones capture Ambient (Environmental) Noise.
2. Processing unit of headphones produces the same signal but in opposite Direction (180° out of phase).
3. Both signals are played together:
    Noise (y₁) + Anti-Noise (−y₁) = 0
4. Ambient noise cancels out and clear Audio is heard.
