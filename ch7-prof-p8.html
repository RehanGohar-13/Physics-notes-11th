Destructive & Stationary Waves
Absent Day 3 · Path difference · Standing waves intro
Destructive interference :-
In this type of interference crest lies of troug. In this way amplitude of wave vanishes.
ΔS = (2n+1) λ/2

In this case path difference is odd integeral multiple of half of wavelength.
Board Check: n = 0, 1, 2… → ΔS = λ/2, 3λ/2, 5λ/2… → amplitude cancels (dark / quiet). Spelling on board “troug / integeral” kept as written; mean trough / integral.
Stationary Waves :-
Superposition of two waves having same frequency and amplitude but travelling in opposite direction. These wave cannot travel or propagates but confined in fixed position. They are also called standing waves.
Confined between boundaries · nodes stay fixed · energy doesn’t propagate forward like a normal travelling wave.
